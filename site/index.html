02 / How it works

Built to run on your machine.

No temp folder full of images. Frames stream from the decoder, through your GPU, into the encoder, so a long video never fills your disk.

  1. 01Import

    Drop a video in. Tween reads its frame rate, length and audio, and suggests targets that fit.

    MP4 · MOV · MKV

  2. 02Analyse

    Hard cuts are found first, so a frame from one shot is never blended into the next.

    Scene-cut detection

  3. 03Interpolate

    RIFE creates the in-between frames on your GPU, with the model loaded once for the whole clip.

    NVIDIA · AMD · Intel · Apple

  4. 04Export

    Hardware encoding when your GPU has it, exact frame count, audio kept in sync.

    NVENC · QSV · x265
